THE PROBLEM

“Good enough”isn’t a measurement.

You know when a shot feels pretty good.

The movement seems smooth. The exposure looks right. The edit works. Maybe you’ve even been paid for it.

But none of that answers the harder question:

How good is the work, actually?

And if it isn’t where you want it to be... what’s holding it back?

YOU CAN KNOW THE MOVES

That doesn’t mean you’re executing them consistently.

YOU CAN KNOW THE SETTINGS

That doesn’t mean your image is controlled.

YOU CAN HAVE CLIENTS

That doesn’t establish the quality of your work.

YOU CAN HAVE EXPERIENCE

That doesn’t guarantee you’re still improving.

You can’t deliberately improve a gap you haven’t learned to see.

FAQ

Questions? Good.

Do I need to already be a professional drone pilot?
No. Getting paid and producing professional-level work are not automatically the same thing. You should, however, already be beyond the true beginner stage. This program develops existing skills rather than teaching basic aircraft operation from zero.
Do I need to already have paying clients?
No. The work is approached through the lens of commercial creative production, but you do not need an existing client roster to participate.
Is this beginner drone training?
No. You should already understand basic aircraft operation, camera settings, exposure, common drone movements, and basic editing.
Is this a business course?
No. We are not focusing on pricing, sales, outreach, lead generation, client acquisition, funnels, or building a drone business. We’re developing the work.
Will you personally review my work?
Yes. For the Founding Cohort, your baseline and final productions will be personally assessed. Questions and developmental feedback throughout the cohort will primarily happen inside the group environment so the entire cohort can learn from what we’re seeing.
Is this a prerecorded video course?
No. The Creative Perspective is a cohort-based development experience built around live instruction, purpose-built demonstrations, critique, assignments, community, and personal assessment.
Is this FPV training?
No. This program is specifically built around DJI GPS-stabilized camera drones. The assignments, assessment framework, and flight instruction are not designed around FPV aircraft.
What if I can’t attend a live session?
Yes. Live sessions will be recorded and made available inside the private cohort community after the session. You’ll be able to go back through the training if you can’t attend live or want to revisit something later.
What drone do I need?
You’ll need a DJI GPS-stabilized camera drone suitable for commercial creative work. As a general minimum, think Mavic 2 generation or newer. This program is specifically built around DJI GPS-stabilized camera drones. It is not FPV training, and the assignments, assessment framework, and flight instruction are not designed around FPV aircraft. If you’re unsure whether your drone qualifies, contact me before enrolling.
What editing software do I need?
You’ll need access to Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve. The editing portion of the program assumes you already understand the basic operation of your chosen software. This is not beginner editing-software training.
How much time should I expect each week?
Plan for approximately 2 hours per week outside of the live training sessions. Most of that time will be spent flying, practicing specific skills, completing assignments, or working with your footage. Some weeks may require slightly more or less time depending on your pace and what you’re working on.
